# EcoTrack AI — Decision Points

## Decision Point 1 — User Nudge Behavior

### Choice: Warn + Encourage

EcoTrack warns users when their footprint is high or when they approach/exceed their weekly target, but it never shames or blocks them. This keeps the application supportive and encourages users to continue tracking their activities and improve their behavior.

---

## Decision Point 2 — Absurd Input Handling

### Choice: Reject / Warn

EcoTrack does not silently calculate clearly unrealistic activity values. Inputs outside reasonable limits are rejected or flagged so that incorrect data does not distort the user's carbon footprint or dashboard.

---

## Decision Point 3 — Week Definition

### Choice: Monday 00:00 → Sunday 23:59

EcoTrack defines a week from Monday through Sunday. Weekly CO₂ progress is calculated using activities logged during the current Monday–Sunday period and compared against the user's configured weekly target.

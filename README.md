# 🌱 EcoTrack AI

> Measure → Understand → Simulate → Act → Track

EcoTrack AI is an AI-powered personal carbon footprint tracking application designed to help users understand and reduce the environmental impact of their everyday activities.

The application allows users to log activities such as transportation, electricity consumption, and meals, calculate their CO₂ footprint using predefined emission factors, monitor their weekly target, analyze historical activity, and receive personalized recommendations from an AI sustainability coach.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

People often want to reduce their environmental impact but do not have a simple way to measure how their everyday activities contribute to their carbon footprint.

EcoTrack AI provides a simple interface where users can record daily activities and immediately understand their estimated CO₂ emissions.

The application transforms raw activity data into:

- 📊 Carbon footprint insights
- 🎯 Weekly targets
- 📈 Historical trends
- 🔎 Category-level breakdowns
- 🤖 Personalized AI recommendations

### 1. 📝 Log an Activity

Supported activities:

- 🚗 Car travel
- 🚌 Bus travel
- ✈️ Flight
- ⚡ Electricity consumption
- 🥗 Vegetarian meal
- 🍗 Non-vegetarian meal
